Corn Downloader privacy

Version 0.8.2 · No telemetry, analytics, advertising or developer servers.

What stays in Firefox

The extension detects media requests on sites where you grant access. Detected URLs, playlist information, selected request headers and download status are kept in memory. The HLS worker count is saved in local extension storage.

What downloads send

When you click Download, Firefox contacts the selected media servers. They receive your IP address, media URLs (which can contain access tokens), and relevant site headers, cookies or authorization information needed to serve the file. Nothing is sent to the developer. The media site's privacy practices apply.

Firefox asks you to accept browsing activity, website content and authentication information permissions during installation or upgrade. These cover downloading from the media site; they do not enable analytics.

Private windows and cleanup

Private-window access is controlled by Firefox. When allowed, downloads use the private download context. HLS requests use captured headers without borrowing cookies from the normal browser session, and do not follow redirects.

Clear, navigation and closing a tab remove its detected media. An active download keeps the information it needs until it finishes or is cancelled. Closing its source tab requests cancellation. Completed job information is then discarded; otherwise it remains until Clear, navigation, tab closure or the next job.

Firefox manages saved files and download history. Private mode does not erase a file you chose to save. Uninstalling the extension does not delete saved videos.

Your controls

Use Cancel to stop a download and Clear to remove detected entries. Manage site access and private-window access in Firefox's extension settings. Direct MP4 downloads from container tabs are not supported; use a normal or private tab.
